# Mayday

An interactive horror narrative made in Twine.

**Play it:** https://mayday.fxasser.com

Your survey ship crashes on an unnamed world. You wake up in a cave with your leg set in a bone splint, and the creature who set it is watching you. She learned to speak from your flight recorder, she thinks your name is "Mayday," and she's very hungry. Over one night in her den, your choices decide whether she sees you as a threat, as prey, or as something she has no word for.

Seven main decisions, five endings, about 5 to 10 minutes per playthrough.

## Running it locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. The `images` folder has to stay next to it.

## Source

`source/Mayday.twee` is the full Twine source. Import it in Twine 2 (Library > Import) with the Harlowe 3 story format.
